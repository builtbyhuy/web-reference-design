02 / The way we work

Good repairs start
with a conversation.

No jargon. No surprise work.
Just the attention your bike deserves.

  1. 01 / TELL US

    What's your bike doing?

    Tell us about the squeak, wobble, or next big ride. You don't need to know the name of the part.

  2. 02 / AGREE IT

    We'll make a plan.

    At drop-off, we check the bike together. You approve the repair and estimate before we pick up a tool.

  3. 03 / RIDE ON

    Back to the good part.

    Collect your bike with a clear explanation of the work and a few tips to keep it running smoothly.
