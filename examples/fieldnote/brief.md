# FIELDNOTE: improve an existing enquiry page

FIELDNOTE is a fictional architecture and interiors studio. This worked example improves a running single page while preserving its business copy, four enquiry fields, three project types and local-preview-only boundary. There are no clients, delivered projects, awards, results, bookings or sending service to imply.

The original fixture uses a fixed-width layout, loses entered values on validation failure, accepts incomplete enquiries and displays user text as HTML. The repair supports a narrow viewport, explicit field errors with retained input, keyboard recovery, a safe local preview and editing without starting again. The preserved fixture is intentionally defective demonstration code; it is not an actual client's website.

## Revised visual direction — 9 October 2026

The first published warm-mineral/Georgia/SVG direction was rejected in visual review. The behavior checks had passed, but that did not establish the quality of its composition, type or art. This revision keeps the established form behavior and core copy and makes a different visual decision: a full-width architectural concept image with strong material texture and depth, a large sans-serif headline, cool white and graphite surfaces, and precise editorial alignment.

The author-supplied Web-reference library was read before selecting references. Its architecture/layout groups 05–06 led to the live [Lapa Ninja architecture gallery](https://www.lapa.ninja/category/architecture/). From there, [Groth Studio](https://groth.studio/) was inspected live on desktop for its assertive sans-serif scale, disciplined grid and fine rules. [HUTS](https://huts.com/) was inspected live on desktop and at 390×844: its first viewport gives architectural media enough scale to establish the subject, while foreground copy and the main action remain clear. At 390px its photograph is recomposed and its headline and action stay grouped. These are mechanisms, not copied identity, source, photos, copy or interaction code. The implementation does not reproduce HUTS' autoplay video or Groth's research sidebar.

### Buildable decisions

- **First viewport:** a quiet white navigation band followed by a full-width, approximately 670–734px-tall image at 1440px. The left-aligned headline and enquiry action sit over the image's shaded threshold. The image's sunlit courtyard, roof plane and olive tree remain visible on the right. A short caption labels the image as an AI-generated concept and the studio as fictional.
- **Type:** self-hosted Manrope. The wordmark is compact and heavy; the headline is medium-weight, tightly spaced and roughly 110px at 1440px. Supporting text is 13–14px with generous line-height. At 390px the headline is about 52px and keeps the two-line phrase. Specific sizes are page decisions, not a general recipe.
- **Color:** cool white `#f7f8f6` and near-black `#171b1d` provide the main surface/text roles. Graphite `#242b2d` supports dark form controls; light pink `#ffb8ac` identifies errors. No gradient or illustration is used to replace missing architectural media; the hero overlay serves foreground readability.
- **Rhythm:** the photograph opens at full scale, a thin caption separates it from the white approach section, and the enquiry becomes a dark chapter. The approach uses a two-column introduction and three ruled service entries rather than a repeated card grid.
- **Mobile:** the navigation retains the primary enquiry action. A 660px-tall hero crops the image at 64% across its width, with a stronger lower shade behind its smaller headline and grouped copy/action. Approach and enquiry stack in reading order; fields and buttons remain full width. No horizontal overflow is concealed.
- **Primary flow:** empty, invalid, ready to preview, preview and edit. Form labels, retained values, summary focus, safe literal rendering and editing behavior stay intact. Nothing is sent or saved.
- **Motion:** static architecture; hover/focus communicate control states. No autoplay, scroll hijacking, hidden entrance content or runtime animation library.

## Incorporated assets and rights

`assets/courtyard-study.webp` is an original AI-generated conceptual architectural visualization made with Codex's built-in image-generation tool on 9 October 2026. It does not depict a delivered FIELDNOTE project. The generated PNG was encoded as a 1672×941 WebP (278,762 bytes) for actual website delivery; the source remains in the host's generated-images folder. Its caption and alternative text state the concept/AI nature. It is supplied under this repository's MIT terms; no third-party architectural photograph or studio identity is copied.

The generation prompt requested an original believable modern coastal residence, seen from a shaded limestone interior threshold toward a sunlit courtyard, still water, a timber soffit and one olive tree. It specified human-eye-level perspective, a quiet dark left third for HTML typography, a luminous right composition supporting a portrait crop, cool Mediterranean morning light, physically plausible structure and editorial material realism. It excluded people, signage, lettering, logos, watermarks, UI, collage and any existing architect's signature building.

`assets/manrope-variable.ttf` is an unmodified Manrope variable font from the official [Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope), downloaded from `Manrope[wght].ttf`. The [actual license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt) was checked on 9 October 2026: SIL Open Font License 1.1, Copyright 2018 The Manrope Project Authors. `assets/manrope-OFL.txt` retains it. The font remains under OFL, not MIT. It is self-hosted, requires no paid plan, account, network request or framework, and falls back to Arial/Helvetica.

The earlier `assets/spatial-study.svg` remains an original MIT asset for history/reuse but is no longer used on this page. [Utopia](https://utopia.fyi/) documentation informed fluid type sizing and [GOV.UK's error summary](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/) informed the functional repair's focusable summary and field links. No code or licensed asset from those sites is embedded here.

## Evidence limits

The recorded original TDD run addresses the concrete form/layout repair. This later visual revision changes CSS, markup and assets while preserving `app.js`; existing meaningful behavior tests are rerun. Passing them cannot prove visual quality, conversion improvement, skill superiority or community adoption. Current desktop/mobile captures and error/preview states are reviewed separately. Browser automation is Chromium emulation, not physical-device or screen-reader evidence.

The strict visual review removed decorative section/service numbers and the hero study badge; enlarged the mobile concept caption to 11px, service descriptions and field labels to 14px; and strengthened tablet/mobile image shade behind the text. The primary phrase remains two lines and the courtyard/roof remain recognizable at narrow width.

The axe scans leave image-background contrast incomplete. A separate render-only check captured the original raster/overlay background after setting only hero foreground text to transparent, using actual text `Range.getClientRects()` rather than the heading container's full width. It sampled the whole line rectangles, including inter-letter pixels. Minimum white/background contrast for the large headline was 3.50, 5.25, 6.86 and 6.86 at 1440, 768, 390 and 320px respectively. The smaller discipline, description and action text was at least 6.44:1 across those views after correction. Before the shade correction, the tablet headline rectangle was 2.46:1 and mobile discipline was 3.21:1; those findings prompted the targeted overlay change. This conservative static rectangle check is not a glyph-mask calculation, other-browser evidence, a complete WCAG audit or a claim about arbitrary image substitutions. The decorative footer arrow inherits `#171b1d` on `#f7f8f6`; it is not the accessible name of the back-to-top link. Current captured images, retained core copy and unchanged form code define the checked scope.
